Our Services

Dundas Valley Pools delivers end-to-end pool solutions for new construction, modernization, equipment reliability, and seasonal upkeep.

New Pool Installation

Site planning, permits, excavation, shell installation, plumbing, electrical, decking, and startup.

Pool Renovation

Liner replacement, resurfacing, tile/coping upgrades, leak mitigation, and structural corrections.

Equipment Upgrades

High-efficiency pumps, cartridge/sand filters, heaters, chlorinators, UV systems, and automation.

Weekly Maintenance

Skimming, brushing, vacuuming, basket cleaning, chemistry balancing, and filter backwashing.

Openings & Closings

Seasonal startup/winterization, cover management, line blowing, and anti-freeze protocols.

Repair Services

Pump motor replacement, heater diagnostics, plumbing repairs, valve replacement, and leak tracing.

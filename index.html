Is your pool liner faded, wrinkled, leaking, or pulling away?

Visible fading, brittle vinyl, tears, wrinkles, staining, or water loss are common signs a liner is nearing end-of-life. If your liner is pulling out of track or leaking, a properly measured replacement can protect the pool structure and restore appearance.

Common warning signs

  • Noticeable fading and staining
  • Wrinkles and shifting vinyl
  • Tears, pinholes, and water loss
  • Liner bead pulling from track
  • Brittle texture or cracking

The Liner Is Only as Good as the Measurement and Install

Even premium liner material can fail early if measurements or prep are rushed. Accurate dimensions, floor/wall prep, and experienced fitting help avoid wrinkles, stress points, and premature wear.

Have Stairs, Benches, or a Complex Pool Shape? We Can Help.

Custom pools, older shells, deep-end slope transitions, integrated steps, and specialty weld sections require tighter tolerances and more installation precision. We plan these details upfront so your liner fits and performs correctly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a pool liner replacement take?

Most jobs include measurement, manufacturing lead time, and a dedicated install window once the liner arrives.

Do you replace liners for complex pool shapes?

Yes, including custom profiles, step sections, benches, and deep-end transitions.

Can I choose the liner pattern?

Absolutely. We help you compare patterns that fit your style and budget.

Do you repair the pool floor before installing the liner?

Yes, prep and repair are addressed as needed to support a proper fit.

When is the best time to replace a liner?

Any time major wear appears. Spring scheduling is common, but planning ahead helps secure preferred timing.

Do I need to be home?

Usually only for key confirmations and access. We keep communication clear throughout the project.
